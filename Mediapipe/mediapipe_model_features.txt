# MediaPipe Model Features

mediapipe_hands_example.py
- Detects: 21 hand landmarks per hand (all 5 fingers, joints, tips)

mediapipe_face_mesh_example.py
- Detects: 468 facial landmarks (eyes, lips, nose, jaw, etc.)
- Supports: Multiple faces

mediapipe_holistic_example.py
- Detects: 33 body pose landmarks, 21 hand landmarks per hand, 468 face mesh landmarks
- All in one pipeline

mediapipe_face_detection_example.py
- Detects: Face bounding boxes (no detailed landmarks)

mediapipe_selfie_segmentation_example.py
- Detects: Person segmentation mask (separates person from background)

mediapipe_all_models_combined.py
- Detects: Body pose, hands, face mesh, face detection, and segmentation (all combined)

custom_upper_body_points.py
- Detects: Custom selection of upper body and hand points (as defined in the script)
